Ridge Riders Cycling Club: December newsletter

What a season. Members logged 48,000 miles between them this year, and 22 riders finished the Valley Century in October. Thank you to everyone who led rides, marshaled at events, and baked for the rest stops.

Winter ride schedule

Group rides switch to winter hours from December 1. All rides leave from the Mill Street bike shop.

Ride Day and time Distance
Coffee spin Saturday, 9 AM 20 miles
Hill repeats Sunday, 8 AM 32 miles
Trail ride Sunday, 10 AM 15 miles

Club news

  • Membership renewals for next year open on December 15. The fee stays at $45.
  • The annual awards dinner is on Saturday, January 16 at the Riverside Grill.
  • Lights are required on all rides that start before sunrise.

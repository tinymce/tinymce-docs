New expense app launches January 4

Posted December 9 by the Finance team

From Monday, January 4, all expense claims move from paper forms and email to the Northwind Expenses app. Claims are approved in about 3 business days instead of 10, and reimbursements arrive with the next payroll run.

Paper forms dated after December 31 cannot be processed. Submit any outstanding paper claims by Friday, December 18.

How to submit a claim

  1. Sign in to the app with your company email address.
  2. Take a photo of each receipt, or forward email receipts to receipts@example.com.
  3. Choose a cost center and select Submit.

Training sessions run every Tuesday in January at 11 AM and 3 PM. Questions? Contact Sam Patel in Finance at extension 4127 or (555) 010-0191.

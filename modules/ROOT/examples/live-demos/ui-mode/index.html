Runbook: renew the VPN certificate

The VPN certificate expires every 12 months. Renew it at least 14 days before the expiry date shown on the VPN dashboard, so that remote staff are not locked out.

DetailValue
Current certificate expiresJanuary 18, 2027
Renewal due byJanuary 4, 2027
OwnerNetwork Engineering

Before you start

  • Book the Sunday maintenance window on the change calendar.
  • Post a notice for remote staff at least two days before the restart.
  • Check that the test laptop has the latest VPN client.

Steps

  1. Create a certificate signing request on the VPN appliance.
  2. Submit the request to the certificate authority and wait for the signed certificate.
  3. Upload the signed certificate and the intermediate chain to the appliance.
  4. Restart the VPN service during the Sunday maintenance window, between 6:00 AM and 8:00 AM.
  5. Connect from a test laptop on a home network to confirm the new certificate.

If the VPN does not accept connections after the restart, upload the previous certificate again and call the network on-call engineer at (555) 010-0199.

Record the new expiry date in the IT calendar and set a reminder for 30 days before it.

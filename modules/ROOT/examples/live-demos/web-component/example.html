<h1>Volunteer guide: Saturday food distribution</h1> <p>Thank you for volunteering with Northside Food Share. Each Saturday about 40 volunteers pack and hand out groceries to more than 250 households.</p> <h2>Before your first shift</h2> <ol> <li>Complete the online orientation (about 20 minutes).</li> <li>Sign the volunteer agreement at the front desk.</li> <li>Wear closed-toe shoes and bring a water bottle.</li> </ol> <h2>Shift times</h2> <table style="border-collapse: collapse; width: 100%;" border="1"> <colgroup><col style="width: 20.2%;"><col style="width: 28.1%;"><col style="width: 51.7%;"></colgroup> <thead> <tr><th>Shift</th><th>Time</th><th>Tasks</th></tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr><td>Setup</td><td>7:30 AM to 9:30 AM</td><td>Unload the truck, set up tables</td></tr> <tr><td>Distribution</td><td>9:30 AM to 12:30 PM</td><td>Greet guests, pack bags, carry groceries</td></tr> <tr><td>Cleanup</td><td>12:30 PM to 2 PM</td><td>Sort leftovers, sweep, stack crates</td></tr> </tbody> </table> <h2>On the day</h2> <ul> <li>Check in with the shift lead at the blue tent.</li> <li>Treat every guest with respect. Do not ask for ID or proof of income.</li> <li>Lift with your legs, and ask for help with boxes over 30 pounds.</li> </ul> <p>Cannot make a shift? Text the volunteer coordinator, Priya Shah, at (555) 010-0119 by Friday at 5 PM.</p>

== Overview

This guide provides instructions for using the comments plugin, including how to:

* xref:add-a-comment[add new comments] to selected content
* xref:replying-to-comments[reply to comments]
* xref:edit-a-comment[edit existing comments]
* xref:delete-a-comment[delete individual comments]
* xref:delete-a-comment-thread-conversation[delete entire conversations]
* xref:resolve-a-comment-thread-conversation[resolve conversations]
* xref:show-or-view-a-comment[view comments] using the sidebar
* xref:delete-all-comment-threads[delete all conversations] in a document

To try these steps, use the xref:introduction-to-tiny-comments.adoc#comments-live-demo[{pluginname} interactive example].

[TIP]
The editor must contain content before a comment can be added.

[[add-a-comment]]
== Add a comment

. Select the content to comment on.
. Start a new comment in one of the following ways:
.. Choose *Insert* -> *Add comment*.
.. Click the *Add comment* image:icons/comment-add.svg[Add comment] toolbar button.
.. Press `Ctrl+Alt+M` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Option+M` (macOS).
. The {pluginname} sidebar opens with an empty comment box. The placeholder text is "_Add comment..._", or "_Comment or mention with @_" when mentions are enabled.
. Type the comment. To add a new line, press *Shift+Enter*.
. Click *Save* or press *Enter* to save the comment, or click *Cancel* to discard it.

*Result*: The selected content is highlighted in the editor, and the comment is displayed in the {pluginname} sidebar.

[[replying-to-comments]]
== Replying to comments

. Select the conversation in the editor or in the {pluginname} sidebar.
. In the conversation, click *Add comment...*.
. Type the reply.
. Click *Save* or press *Enter* to save the reply, or click *Cancel* to discard it.

[[edit-a-comment]]
== Edit a comment

. Click the ellipsis image:icons/image-options.svg[(ellipsis - 3 horizontal dots)] icon on the comment to open the menu.
. Select *Edit*.
. Change the comment, then click *Save*, or click *Cancel* to discard the changes.

[[delete-a-comment]]
== Delete a comment

This option is available for replies only. To delete the first comment in a conversation, xref:delete-a-comment-thread-conversation[delete the conversation].

. Click the ellipsis image:icons/image-options.svg[(ellipsis - 3 horizontal dots)] icon on the comment to open the menu.
. Select *Delete*.
. In the *Delete Comment* dialog, click *Delete* to delete the comment, or *Cancel* to keep it.

[[delete-a-comment-thread-conversation]]
== Delete a comment thread (conversation)

This option is available on the first comment in a conversation only.

. Click the ellipsis image:icons/image-options.svg[(ellipsis - 3 horizontal dots)] icon on the first comment to open the menu.
. Select *Delete conversation*.
. The *Delete Conversation* dialog states how many comments will be deleted. Click *Delete* to delete the conversation, or *Cancel* to keep it.

*Result*: The conversation and all of its comments are deleted from the document.

[[resolve-a-comment-thread-conversation]]
== Resolve a comment thread (conversation)

This option is available on the first comment in a conversation only, and requires the xref:comments-callback-mode.adoc#tinycomments_resolve[`+tinycomments_resolve+`] option in callback mode or the xref:comments-embedded-mode.adoc#tinycomments_can_resolve[`+tinycomments_can_resolve+`] option in embedded mode.

. Click the ellipsis image:icons/image-options.svg[(ellipsis - 3 horizontal dots)] icon on the first comment to open the menu.
. Select *Resolve conversation*.
. The *Resolve Conversation* dialog states how many comments will be resolved. Click *Resolve* to resolve the conversation, or *Cancel* to keep it open.

*Result*: The conversation is removed from the {pluginname} sidebar, and its highlight is removed from the content. In embedded mode, the conversation is removed from the content. In callback mode, the `+tinycomments_resolve+` callback determines how the resolved conversation is stored.

[[show-or-view-a-comment]]
== Show or view a comment

. Place the cursor in highlighted content in the editor.
. Choose *View* -> *Show comments*, or click the *Show comments* image:icons/comment.svg[Show comments] toolbar button.

*Result*: The {pluginname} sidebar opens and displays the conversation for the highlighted content.

[[delete-all-comment-threads]]
== Delete all comment threads

. Choose *File* -> *Delete all conversations*.
. A dialog asks "_Delete all conversations in the content? This cannot be undone._" Click *Yes* to delete all conversations in the document, or *No* to cancel.

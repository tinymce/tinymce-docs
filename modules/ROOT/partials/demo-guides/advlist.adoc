<div class="demo-guide demo-guide--opensource" role="note" aria-label="Try it: List Styles">
  <div class="demo-guide__head">
    <span class="demo-guide__eyebrow">Try it</span>
    <span class="demo-guide__title">List Styles</span>
    <span class="demo-guide__tier">Open source plugin</span>
  </div>
  <div class="demo-guide__body">
    <p class="demo-guide__task">Articles I and II of this airline baggage policy follow the outline style: Roman numerals, then capital letters, then lowercase letters. Article III was pasted in with plain numbers in its 2 nested lists. Restyle them to match.</p>
    <ol class="demo-guide__steps">
      <li>Place the insertion point in the first item under <strong>Delayed and damaged baggage</strong>, open the arrow next to <span class="demo-ui">Numbered list</span> and choose <span class="demo-ui">Upper Alpha</span>.</li>
      <li>Place the insertion point in <strong>$50 per day for the first five days</strong> and choose <span class="demo-ui">Lower Alpha</span> from the same menu. The menu offers 5 styles, in the order the outline uses them.</li>
      <li>Place the insertion point in the first item under <strong>Schedule B</strong>, open the arrow next to <span class="demo-ui">Bullet list</span> and switch between the 2 bullet styles, <span class="demo-ui">Square</span> and <span class="demo-ui">Disc</span>.</li>
      <li>Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> in a list item to nest it one level deeper, then give the new level its own style.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <details class="demo-guide__config">
    <summary class="demo-guide__label">Configured in this demo</summary>
    <ul class="demo-guide__options">
      <li><code>advlist_number_styles: 'default,upper-roman,upper-alpha,lower-alpha,lower-roman'</code> lists the numbering styles in outline order and leaves out <strong>Lower Greek</strong>.</li>
      <li><code>advlist_bullet_styles: 'square,disc'</code> limits the bullet styles to the two that the policy uses.</li>
    </ul>
  </details>
</div>

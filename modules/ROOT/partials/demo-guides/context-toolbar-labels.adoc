The toolbar shows three groups: the alignment buttons, a group with the visible label <strong>Copy</strong> and the <span class="demo-ui">Copy</span> and <span class="demo-ui">Paste</span> buttons, and <span class="demo-ui">Bold</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Italic</span>, and <span class="demo-ui">Underline</span>.</li>

      <li>Move through a toolbar with a screen reader to hear the group names <strong>Formatting</strong> and <strong>Format</strong>. A <code>name</code> is announced but not shown, while a <code>label</code> is shown as text.</li>

    <summary class="demo-guide__label">Configured in this demo</summary>
    <ul class="demo-guide__options">
      <li>The <code>imagealignment</code> context toolbar sets <code>items</code> to two groups: <code>name: 'Formatting'</code> for the alignment buttons, and <code>label: 'Copy'</code> for <code>copy</code> and <code>paste</code>.</li>
      <li>The <code>textselection</code> context toolbar has one group, <code>name: 'Format'</code>, with <code>bold</code>, <code>italic</code>, and <code>underline</code>. It appears whenever the selection is not collapsed, which includes a selected image.</li>
      <li>A group <code>name</code> becomes the group&#8217;s accessible name. A group <code>label</code> also becomes the accessible name, and is shown before the buttons.</li>
    </ul>

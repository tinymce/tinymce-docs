    <span class="demo-guide__title">Editable areas in locked content</span>
    <span class="demo-guide__tier">Core feature</span>

  <div class="demo-guide__body">
    <p class="demo-guide__task">This services agreement template is locked, except for 3 highlighted areas: the client details, the start date, and the special terms. Fill in the agreement for a new client.</p>
    <ol class="demo-guide__steps">
      <li>Click in a numbered clause, such as <strong>4. Confidentiality</strong>. The text cannot be changed, and formatting buttons such as <span class="demo-ui">Bold</span> and <span class="demo-ui">Italic</span> are unavailable.</li>
      <li>Click in the highlighted <strong>Client</strong> line and replace the client name and address.</li>
      <li>Change the <strong>Start date</strong>, then add a second paragraph under <strong>5. Special terms</strong>. Formatting buttons are available inside the highlighted areas.</li>
      <li>Select <span class="demo-ui">Source code</span> in the toolbar, or open <span class="demo-ui">View</span> &gt; <span class="demo-ui">Source code</span>. The areas keep <code>class="editable"</code>, and no <code>contenteditable</code> attribute is saved.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <details class="demo-guide__config">
    <summary class="demo-guide__label">Configured in this demo</summary>
    <ul class="demo-guide__options">
      <li><code>editable_root: false</code> makes the whole document non-editable.</li>
      <li><code>editable_class: 'editable'</code> makes elements with the <code>editable</code> class editable again, in place of the default <code>mceEditable</code> class.</li>
      <li><code>content_style</code> highlights the editable areas with a dashed outline.</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

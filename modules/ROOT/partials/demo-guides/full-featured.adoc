  <details class="demo-guide__config">
    <summary class="demo-guide__label">Configured in this demo</summary>
    <ul class="demo-guide__options">
      <li><code>tinycomments_mode: 'callback'</code> with the <code>tinycomments_*</code> callbacks stores comments in a local sample database.</li>
      <li><code>revisionhistory_fetch</code> loads three sample revisions, and <code>revisionhistory_display_author</code> shows who saved each one.</li>
      <li><code>mentions_fetch</code> and the other <code>mentions_*</code> options look up people in a sample user directory when typing <code>@</code>.</li>
      <li><code>mergetags_list</code> sets the Client, Proposal, Consultant and Salutation merge tags, and <code>advtemplate_templates</code> sets the <strong>Resolving tickets</strong> and <strong>Quick replies</strong> templates.</li>
      <li><code>sidebar_show: 'tinymceai-chat'</code> opens the AI Chat sidebar on load, and the <code>tinymceai_*</code> options set its token provider, sources, custom quick action, and translation languages.</li>
      <li><code>menu</code> and <code>menubar</code> add a <strong>Comments</strong> menu, and <code>toolbar_mode: 'sliding'</code> puts the toolbar buttons that do not fit behind the <span class="demo-ui">Reveal or hide additional toolbar items</span> button.</li>
      <li><code>uploadcare_*</code> options configure image and video uploads with Media Optimizer.</li>
      <li><code>exportpdf_converter_options</code> and <code>exportword_converter_options</code> export on US Letter paper.</li>
      <li><code>typography_*</code>, <code>autocorrect_capitalize</code>, <code>autosave_*</code>, and <code>spellchecker_ignore_list</code> set typography rules, capitalization, autosave, and words the spell checker ignores, such as {productname}.</li>
    </ul>

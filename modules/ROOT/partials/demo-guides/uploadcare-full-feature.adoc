      <li>Select the kitchen photo to open its toolbar: <span class="demo-ui">Align</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Transform</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Adjust</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Filters</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Alt text</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Caption</span> and <span class="demo-ui">Revert</span>.</li>

      <li>Play the aerial video with its player controls, or drag a corner handle to resize it. To add a video, place the insertion point in an empty paragraph, select <span class="demo-ui">Insert video</span>, then <span class="demo-ui">Upload from device</span> or <span class="demo-ui">Upload from URL</span>.</li>
      <li>Select the words <strong>Floor plan</strong>, select <span class="demo-ui">Insert/edit link</span> and open the <span class="demo-ui">Upload</span> tab. Drop a PDF or select <span class="demo-ui">Browse for a file</span>, then select <span class="demo-ui">Save</span>.</li>
    </ol>

  <details class="demo-guide__config">
    <summary class="demo-guide__label">Configured in this demo</summary>
    <ul class="demo-guide__options">
      <li><code>uploadcare_filters</code> fills <strong>Choose Filter</strong> with 9 entries: <strong>Adaris</strong>, <strong>Vintage</strong>, <strong>Base</strong>, <strong>Light</strong>, <strong>Intense</strong>, <strong>Soft</strong>, <strong>Sharp</strong>, <strong>Night</strong> and <strong>None</strong>.</li>
      <li><code>uploadcare_video_properties</code> gives the aerial video, and every new video, player controls, muted sound, metadata-only preloading, a poster frame taken at 10 seconds and a fluid width.</li>
      <li><code>documents_file_types</code> limits the <strong>Upload</strong> tab to 9 Office, PDF, RTF and plain-text types.</li>
      <li><code>a11y_advanced_options: true</code> adds the <strong>Decorative image</strong> toggle to the <strong>Alt text</strong> form.</li>
      <li><code>uploadcare_store_type: 'temporary'</code> stores files uploaded in this demo temporarily: Uploadcare removes them after 24 hours.</li>
      <li><code>uploadcare_public_key</code> connects the editor to the Uploadcare project that stores and serves the media.</li>
    </ul>

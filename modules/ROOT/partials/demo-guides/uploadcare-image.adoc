<div class="demo-guide demo-guide--premium" role="note" aria-label="Try it: Media Optimizer image editing">
  <div class="demo-guide__head">
    <span class="demo-guide__eyebrow">Try it</span>
    <span class="demo-guide__title">Media Optimizer: images</span>
    <span class="demo-guide__tier">Premium plugin</span>
  </div>
  <div class="demo-guide__body">
    <p class="demo-guide__task">This photographer's portfolio has 4 photos. Prepare the <strong>Pass road in June</strong> photo for its black-and-white print, warm up the lake photo, and caption the boardwalk photo. Every edit is non-destructive.</p>
    <ol class="demo-guide__steps">
      <li>Select the mountain road photo to open its toolbar: <span class="demo-ui">Align</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Transform</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Adjust</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Filters</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Alt text</span>, <span class="demo-ui">Caption</span> and <span class="demo-ui">Revert</span>. The first edit copies this photo to Uploadcare, so it can take a moment.</li>
      <li>Select <span class="demo-ui">Adjust</span>, then <span class="demo-ui">Grayscale</span>. Select <span class="demo-ui">Back</span>, then <span class="demo-ui">Transform</span> to rotate, flip or resize the photo.</li>
      <li>Select the lake photo, then <span class="demo-ui">Adjust</span> &gt; <span class="demo-ui">Warmth</span>, and drag the slider. <span class="demo-ui">Reset</span> clears that one adjustment. Under <span class="demo-ui">Filters</span>, try <span class="demo-ui">Auto enhance</span> or a filter from <span class="demo-ui">Choose Filter</span>.</li>
      <li>Select the boardwalk photo and select <span class="demo-ui">Caption</span>. A caption appears under the photo, filled in from its alt text, ready to edit.</li>
      <li>Select <span class="demo-ui">Revert</span> on any edited photo to restore the original. To add a photo, place the insertion point in an empty paragraph and select <span class="demo-ui">Insert image</span>.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <details class="demo-guide__config">
    <summary class="demo-guide__label">Configured in this demo</summary>
    <ul class="demo-guide__options">
      <li><code>uploadcare_filters</code> fills <strong>Choose Filter</strong> with 9 entries: <strong>Adaris</strong>, <strong>Vintage</strong>, <strong>Base</strong>, <strong>Light</strong>, <strong>Intense</strong>, <strong>Soft</strong>, <strong>Sharp</strong>, <strong>Night</strong> and <strong>None</strong>.</li>
      <li><code>a11y_advanced_options: true</code> adds the <strong>Decorative image</strong> toggle to the <strong>Alt text</strong> form.</li>
      <li><code>uploadcare_store_type: 'temporary'</code> stores files uploaded in this demo temporarily: Uploadcare removes them after 24 hours.</li>
      <li><code>uploadcare_public_key</code> connects the editor to the Uploadcare project that stores and serves the media.</li>
    </ul>
  </details>
</div>
